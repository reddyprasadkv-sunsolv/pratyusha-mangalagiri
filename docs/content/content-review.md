# Crystal Sales Content Review

## Review outcome

The source has a clear commercial idea: Pratyusha personally selects crystal bracelets and pairs them with a complimentary 21-day guided practice centred on mindfulness, intention-setting, affirmations, gratitude, journaling, positive habits, and consistent personal action.

The strongest differentiator is not a guaranteed crystal outcome. It is the combination of a physical bracelet, personal guidance, and a structured daily ritual. The public message should keep that distinction while removing or qualifying unverified scale, scarcity, material, delivery, credential, and outcome claims.

Recommended primary hero direction: **Transform Your Energy. Transform Your Life.**

Recommended supporting positioning: **Handpicked crystal bracelets with personal guidance and a complimentary 21-Day Crystal Healing Ritual.**

The second concept, **Heal Your Energy. Heal Your Life.**, should not repeat the hero. If retained after wellness review, it may introduce the customer-challenge section with claim-safe language about mindfulness and emotional balance.

## Content strengths

- A recognisable headline with a simple transformation arc.
- A differentiated offer that goes beyond the bracelet.
- Four named products with distinct intention-led positioning.
- A structured 21-day experience that can support daily consistency.
- A clear beginner-friendly audience.
- A five-step journey that is easy to understand on mobile.
- Four useful starter FAQs.
- An appropriate emphasis on intention, mindset, and practical action in the source author note.
- A personal founder-led voice rather than an anonymous retail tone.

## Future Angular content-model preparation

### Existing model assessment

The current Angular `PublicPageCopy` is a locale-specific, presentation-shaped object for the existing business-services page. It already supports typed locale selection, signals, SSR-safe language persistence, and a central content service. Crystal content should reuse that infrastructure but should not be forced into generic `NumberedContent` arrays or hardcoded into templates.

Recommended approach after English approval:

- Keep `SupportedLanguage = 'en' | 'te'` and the existing locale service.
- Add content lifecycle metadata so unapproved items cannot be published accidentally.
- Represent products, ritual inclusions, FAQs, contact details, SEO, and legal copy as separate typed entities.
- Store stable IDs independently from translated labels.
- Keep optional operational fields absent until confirmed rather than inserting placeholders.
- Use an explicit `published` flag and status gate for testimonials, products, contact methods, and claims.
- Map approved English and Telugu values through `BilingualText`; do not auto-translate at runtime.

### Recommended documentation interfaces

```ts
type ContentStatus =
  | 'APPROVED'
  | 'DRAFT'
  | 'CLIENT CONFIRMATION REQUIRED'
  | 'EVIDENCE REQUIRED'
  | 'LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED'
  | 'WELLNESS REVIEW REQUIRED'
  | 'REMOVE'
  | 'NOT PROVIDED';
```

### Mapping notes

- `HeroContent`: hero option selected by the client after English approval.
- `ProductContent`: one record per bracelet; do not publish without material, price, size, image, and order confirmation.
- `RitualContent`: inclusion records remain unpublished until delivery is confirmed.
- `BenefitContent`: supportive, non-guaranteed outcomes only.
- `ProcessStep`: stable five-step journey; steps may change after ordering-flow confirmation.
- `FAQContent`: questions may exist without answers while status is `CLIENT CONFIRMATION REQUIRED`.
- `ContactContent`: optional fields prevent placeholder values from reaching the UI.
- `SeoContent`: English first; Telugu only after approved human translation.
- `LegalContent`: all policy/disclaimer content remains unpublished until the relevant review is complete.

No Supabase connection or Angular implementation is part of Step 3A.

## Quality review

- **Repetition:** consolidated through the 20-section structure.
- **Grammar/readability:** sentences shortened and customer-facing language simplified.
- **Conversion flow:** challenge → support → products → ritual → fit → difference → process → founder → proof → FAQ → disclaimer → CTA.
- **Claim safety:** guaranteed financial, medical, career, relationship, and protection outcomes are not included in the draft.
- **CTA clarity:** CTAs match the readiness of ordering information and do not use false urgency.
- **Tone:** premium, warm, personal, and practical.
- **Product naming:** traditional names retained; descriptions qualified.
- **Mobile readiness:** short paragraphs, scannable cards, and concise headings.
- **Telugu readiness:** ambiguous claims and operational gaps are flagged before translation.
