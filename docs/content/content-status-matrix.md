# Crystal Sales Content Status Matrix

## Status vocabulary

Only these statuses may be used for content decisions:

- **APPROVED** — explicitly approved for the stated use
- **DRAFT** — prepared for review; not approved for publication
- **CLIENT CONFIRMATION REQUIRED** — fact, offer, process, or preference must be confirmed
- **EVIDENCE REQUIRED** — objective claim requires reliable substantiation
- **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** — policy, regulated, or liability-sensitive wording needs legal review
- **WELLNESS REVIEW REQUIRED** — crystal, healing, energy, emotional, or outcome wording needs specialist review
- **REMOVE** — do not publish in the current form
- **NOT PROVIDED** — source or approval has not been supplied

No customer-facing crystal content is marked **APPROVED** in Step 3A.

## Publication rule

An item may become **APPROVED** only after the named approval gate is completed and recorded. A future Angular implementation should render only records with `published: true` and the required approval status. No Step 3A document changes the current public UI.
