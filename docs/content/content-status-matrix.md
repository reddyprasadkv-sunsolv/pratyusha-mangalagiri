## Status vocabulary

- **APPROVED** — explicitly approved for the stated use
- **CLIENT REVIEW DRAFT** — polished bilingual candidate; not approved for publication
- **CLIENT CONFIRMATION REQUIRED** — a fact, offer, process, or preference must be confirmed
- **EVIDENCE REQUIRED** — an objective claim requires reliable substantiation
- **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** — policy, regulated, or liability-sensitive wording needs legal review
- **WELLNESS REVIEW REQUIRED** — crystal, healing, energy, emotional, or outcome wording needs specialist review
- **LEGAL AND CLIENT REVIEW REQUIRED** — both legal and client approval are mandatory
- **NOT READY TO PUBLISH** — operational or approval dependencies prevent publication
- **KEEP HIDDEN** — section must not render publicly
- **REMOVE** — do not publish in the current form
- **NOT PROVIDED** — source or approval has not been supplied

No crystal-sales content is marked **APPROVED** in Step 3B.
