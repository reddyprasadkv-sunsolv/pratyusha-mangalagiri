# Telugu Translation Guidelines for the Crystal Sales Page

## Status and scope

- Status: **DRAFT — CLIENT APPROVAL REQUIRED**
- Full Telugu translation: **NOT PROVIDED in Step 3A**
- Translation may begin only after the English content, claims, product facts, contact details, and policies are approved.

The Telugu version must carry the same approved meaning and the same limitations as the English version. It must not strengthen a supportive statement into a promise, add a benefit, or fill an English information gap.

## Translation principles

The Telugu should be:

- Natural and conversational
- Simple enough for normal Telugu readers
- Warm, respectful, and reassuring
- Premium without sounding ceremonial or overly formal
- Written for meaning, not translated word for word
- Consistent with the approved English message
- Free from difficult literary vocabulary
- Comfortable with familiar English product or digital terms where that improves clarity
- Easy to scan on a mobile screen

## Voice and tone

- Address the reader respectfully and directly.
- Prefer short sentences with one idea each.
- Use everyday verbs and familiar sentence order.
- Avoid exaggerated spiritual language.
- Avoid fear-based language around protection, money, health, or negative energy.
- Keep Pratyusha’s personal voice warm, not authoritative or clinical.
- Describe the ritual as a guided practice, not a treatment programme.
- Encourage reflection and practical action together.

## Meaning rules

### Preserve supportive qualifiers

English qualifiers such as these must remain equally clear in Telugu:

- may support
- designed to support
- can serve as a reminder
- intended to complement
- encourages
- helps create a consistent practice
- individual experiences may vary

Do not translate them into phrases meaning “will give,” “will bring,” “will cure,” “will remove,” “will protect,” or “will ensure.”

### Preserve non-guarantee boundaries

The Telugu version must not state or imply that a bracelet:

- guarantees wealth or income
- guarantees career, academic, or business success
- treats anxiety, stress, depression, or another health condition
- guarantees a relationship outcome
- protects the wearer from every negative event or person
- guarantees a fixed personal transformation

### Do not fill missing content

If English status is **NOT PROVIDED**, **CLIENT CONFIRMATION REQUIRED**, **EVIDENCE REQUIRED**, **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED**, or **WELLNESS REVIEW REQUIRED**, do not invent a Telugu answer. Carry the same status forward.

## Product names

Traditional/commercial product names should remain stable across languages until the client approves a naming policy.

Recommended approach:

- Keep the approved English product name visible.
- Add a short, natural Telugu explanation only after the English positioning is approved.
- Do not translate “Money Magnet” into a Telugu phrase that promises money attraction.
- Do not translate “Evil Eye Protection” into a phrase that promises complete protection.
- Present “Pyrite” as the material/product name; do not invent local mineral claims.
- Use the same display order and stable product ID in both languages.

## Headline guidance

- Keep the emotional force of the approved English headline without making the outcome more certain.
- A short English brand line may remain if the client wants bilingual visual continuity, but the Telugu meaning must be nearby and understandable.
- Avoid mechanically repeating “energy,” “life,” “change,” or “healing” across several headings.
- Keep mobile headings to roughly two or three short lines at common widths.
- Use sentence case; avoid long all-capital English strings inside Telugu content.

## Body-copy guidance

- Prefer paragraphs of one to three sentences.
- Break long inclusion lists into short, parallel phrases.
- Keep subjects and actions explicit; avoid vague spiritual claims.
- Use familiar English words only where they are more recognisable than a formal Telugu equivalent.
- Do not add excessive English inside a Telugu sentence.
- Maintain the distinction between product name, traditional association, intended practice, and guaranteed result.

## CTA guidance

CTAs should be direct and low-pressure. Translate the approved action, not just the words.

Examples of CTA intent to preserve:

- Explore the Crystal Collection
- Choose Your Crystal
- Begin Your Crystal Journey
- Discover the 21-Day Ritual
- Ask About a Bracelet
- Send an Enquiry
- Order Through WhatsApp — only after the channel is confirmed

Do not introduce urgency words equivalent to “today only,” “last chance,” or “limited stock” without evidence and English approval. Do not use the equivalent of “Buy Now” until price and checkout/order readiness are confirmed.

## Credentials and founder content

- Do not translate or transliterate a professional title until the exact English title is verified.
- Preserve certificate/institution names exactly if supplied.
- Do not convert a practitioner title into “doctor,” “therapist,” “counsellor,” or another regulated role.
- Keep the founder biography consistent with confirmed experience and scope.
- Do not add honorifics or qualifications not present in the approved English.

## Product and material facts

- Translate natural/synthetic/treatment disclosures precisely.
- Use consistent terms for bead size, metal, coating, and care.
- Keep allergy and skin-sensitivity warnings direct and readable.
- Do not imply all crystals are natural if only some are confirmed.
- Preserve measurements, currency, prices, sizes, delivery ranges, and policy periods exactly.

## Wellness disclaimer rules

The approved Telugu disclaimer must clearly communicate that:

- crystals and the ritual are for mindfulness, intention-setting, reflection, and personal-wellness practices
- they are not medical treatments
- they are not substitutes for medical, psychological, legal, or financial advice
- experiences vary
- no financial, health, career, protection, business, or relationship result is guaranteed
- practical action and qualified professional support should be used where needed

The disclaimer should sound calm and professional, not frightening. It requires independent Telugu proofreading plus client, wellness, and legal approval.

## FAQ translation rules

- Translate only approved answers.
- Keep unapproved questions visible in the content workflow, not as invented public answers.
- The natural-crystal answer must match confirmed material disclosures.
- Shipping, Cash on Delivery, returns, damage, size, and ritual-delivery answers must match current operations.
- Use identical policy time periods and eligibility conditions in both languages.

## SEO translation rules

- Telugu SEO is a separate writing task, not a literal conversion of English metadata.
- Preserve the approved page purpose and primary product concepts.
- Use natural search language without keyword stuffing.
- Do not add a city, state, India-wide delivery, “natural,” “certified,” price, or availability claim unless approved.
- Keep title and description within practical search-result lengths after Telugu rendering.
- Prepare Telugu Open Graph copy only after the English social message and image are approved.

## Typography and Unicode readiness

- Use Unicode Telugu text only.
- Use Noto Sans Telugu for body copy.
- Use Noto Serif Telugu only for selected approved premium headings or quotations.
- Allow generous Telugu line height so vowel signs and conjuncts do not clip.
- Do not apply letter spacing to Telugu body text.
- Test mixed Telugu/English strings, numbers, currency, punctuation, and parentheses.
- Confirm wrapping at 320, 360, 375, 390, 414, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440, and 1920 pixels during the later UI step.
- Check buttons, tabs, accordions, form errors, and metadata for overflow.
- Keep stable content IDs and route behaviour separate from translated labels.

## Translation workflow

1. Approve the English section and status.
2. Resolve every client, evidence, wellness, and legal dependency.
3. Freeze the English meaning for translation.
4. Prepare a human Telugu draft in the typed content model.
5. Perform a meaning comparison against English.
6. Review claim strength line by line.
7. Ask a normal Telugu reader to check simplicity and naturalness.
8. Proofread spelling, punctuation, terminology, and mixed-script text.
9. Complete client, wellness, and legal review where required.
10. Mark Telugu content **APPROVED** only after approval is recorded.

## Translation QA checklist

- [ ] Meaning matches approved English
- [ ] No new service, property, credential, testimonial, statistic, or guarantee
- [ ] Supportive qualifiers retained
- [ ] No medical or psychological treatment implication
- [ ] No guaranteed financial, protection, career, business, or relationship result
- [ ] Product names consistent
- [ ] Ritual inclusions and delivery methods exact
- [ ] Contact, price, size, shipping, and policy details exact
- [ ] Natural/synthetic/material disclosures exact
- [ ] Telugu sounds conversational, not literal
- [ ] Difficult vocabulary simplified
- [ ] Paragraphs and headings are mobile-friendly
- [ ] Unicode and mixed-script rendering checked
- [ ] English and Telugu statuses match
- [ ] Client approval recorded
- [ ] Wellness/legal approval recorded where required

## Angular content note

The existing Angular locale service and routes can support the future translation. Store approved English and Telugu in typed `BilingualText` values with stable IDs. Do not use runtime machine translation. Missing Telugu values must remain unpublished rather than falling back to unapproved generated text.
