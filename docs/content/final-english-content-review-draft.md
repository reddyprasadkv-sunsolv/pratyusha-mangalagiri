- `HeroContent`: hero heading, support, body, CTAs, image reference, status
- `ProductContent`: four stable product IDs, bilingual copy, materials, sizes, price, stock, image, CTA, disclaimer, publication flag
- `RitualContent`: overview plus inclusion records with delivery, frequency, duration, and confirmed status
- `BenefitContent`: claim-safe support points
- `ProcessStep`: five ordered journey steps
- `FounderContent`: approved biography, portrait, and separately verified credentials
- `FAQContent`: approved answers or unpublished pending questions
- `ContactContent`: optional verified contact and ordering fields; no public placeholders
- `SeoContent`: title, description, H1, Open Graph content, and image alt text
- `DisclaimerContent`: matched English/Telugu disclaimer with approval metadata

Store English and Telugu in separate fields such as `title_en`, `title_te`, `description_en`, `description_te`, `button_text_en`, `button_text_te`, `alt_text_en`, and `alt_text_te`. Stable IDs and approval states allow the same records to be loaded later from Supabase without changing the Angular presentation structure. Step 3B does not connect Supabase or modify Angular code.
